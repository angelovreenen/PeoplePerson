Privacy Policy

Your information is handled with care

People Person respects confidentiality and takes reasonable steps to protect client information. We only use information provided to us to support our advisory services and to communicate with clients responsibly and professionally.

We may collect details such as your name, company, contact information, and service requirements for the purpose of engagement, support, and communication. Information is not shared with third parties except where required for service delivery, legal compliance, or explicit client consent.

We maintain secure and separate operational processes where possible and apply appropriate safeguards to protect confidential information. Clients may contact us at any time to request information about how their data is being handled.
