Frequently Asked

Clear answers regarding our engagements

Our flagship retainer includes a flexible allocation of 20 (twenty) hours per calendar month, which can be distributed across any combination of HR advisory, compliance, system pipeline deployment, or training functions selected by the Client.
To ensure high-quality delivery and dedicated focus, primary service execution and administrative tasks are performed during dedicated consulting blocks—typically after 17:00 SAST on weekdays and during weekends. Brief urgent telephonic or email advisory is available during standard business hours.
All service requests and management tasks are submitted via your preferred digital platform or email to ensure a transparent, automated audit trail of the hours utilized. This gives you complete visibility over your monthly hour allocations.
Data segregation is critical. We maintain completely independent infrastructure and equipment for all "People Person" operations to guarantee total isolation, partitioning, and absolute confidentiality of your corporate records.
