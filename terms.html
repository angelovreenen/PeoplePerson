Terms of Engagement

Clear engagement terms for clearer partnerships

Our engagement model is designed to be transparent and practical. Work is scoped according to client need, service type, and agreed retainer or project structure.

Client work is typically delivered within an agreed scope, timeline, and communication structure. Retainer services are based on planned monthly capacity, with additional requests handled according to agreed priorities and process.

Information, recommendations, and advice are delivered as professional advisory support and should be reviewed in the context of each client’s legal and operational framework. Clients remain responsible for implementation decisions, operational execution, and internal governance.
